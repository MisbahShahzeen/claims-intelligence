# HOUSEHOLDER PROPERTY POLICY - STANDARD WORDING
Version 2 | Effective 1 April 2026

## SECTION 1 - BUILDING AND CONTENTS

### 1.1 Insured perils
The Company shall indemnify the Insured against loss of or damage to the
insured building and contents caused by fire, lightning, explosion, aircraft
damage, riot, strike, malicious damage, storm, cyclone, flood, inundation,
impact by vehicle, subsidence, landslide, bursting of water tanks or pipes,
and missile testing operations.

### 1.2 Burglary and housebreaking
Loss of contents by burglary or housebreaking is covered where entry to the
premises was effected by forcible and violent means, and where a First
Information Report has been lodged with the police.

### 1.3 Basis of settlement
Building claims are settled on reinstatement value. Contents claims are settled
on market value at the time of loss, less depreciation, unless the Schedule
records agreed value cover for the item.

## SECTION 2 - EXCLUSIONS

### 2.1 Unoccupied premises
No claim shall be payable for loss occurring while the premises have been left
unoccupied for a continuous period exceeding thirty days, unless the Company
has been notified in writing and has agreed to continue cover.

### 2.2 Gradual deterioration
Loss or damage arising from wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rust,
corrosion, dampness, dry rot, fungus, insects, or vermin is excluded.

### 2.3 Valuables without declaration
Jewellery, precious stones, bullion, curios, works of art, and manuscripts are
excluded unless specifically declared in the Schedule with agreed values.

### 2.4 Money and securities
Cash, bank notes, cheques, and negotiable instruments are excluded from
contents cover.

### 2.5 Defective workmanship
Loss arising from faulty design, defective workmanship, or the use of defective
materials is excluded, but resulting damage to other insured property is
covered.

## SECTION 3 - CLAIMS PROCEDURE

### 3.1 Notification and evidence
Claims must be notified within seven days. The Insured shall provide purchase
receipts, valuation certificates, or other reasonable proof of ownership and
value for items claimed.

### 3.2 Mitigation
The Insured shall take all reasonable steps to prevent further loss following
an insured event. Costs reasonably incurred in doing so are payable.
