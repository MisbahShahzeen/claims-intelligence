# MOTOR PRIVATE CAR POLICY - STANDARD WORDING
Version 3 | Effective 1 January 2026

## SECTION 1 - OWN DAMAGE COVER

### 1.1 Scope of cover
The Company shall indemnify the Insured against loss of or damage to the
insured vehicle arising from accidental external means, including collision
and overturning, fire, self-ignition, lightning, explosion, burglary,
housebreaking or theft, riot and strike, malicious act, terrorist activity,
earthquake, flood, storm, cyclone, inundation, or damage in transit by road,
rail, inland waterway, lift, elevator or air.

### 1.2 Basis of settlement
Settlement shall be on the basis of reasonable repair cost, subject to
depreciation applied to replaced parts in accordance with the schedule at
Section 6, and shall not exceed the Insured's Declared Value.

### 1.3 Deductible
The deductible stated in the Schedule is borne by the Insured on every claim
under this Section. Where multiple items are damaged in a single event, one
deductible applies to the event and not to each item.

## SECTION 2 - THIRD PARTY LIABILITY

### 2.1 Death or bodily injury
The Company shall indemnify the Insured against legal liability for death of
or bodily injury to any third party arising out of the use of the insured
vehicle, without monetary limit.

### 2.2 Third party property damage
Liability for damage to third party property is covered up to the limit stated
in the Schedule.

## SECTION 3 - EXCLUSIONS

### 3.1 Consequential loss and depreciation
The Company shall not be liable for consequential loss, depreciation, wear and
tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, failure or breakage.

### 3.2 Unlicensed driver
No claim shall be payable where the vehicle was being driven by a person not
holding a valid and effective driving licence for that class of vehicle at the
time of the loss.

### 3.3 Intoxication
No claim shall be payable where the person driving the vehicle was under the
influence of alcohol or drugs at the time of the loss.

### 3.4 Use outside limitations
No claim shall be payable where the loss occurred while the vehicle was used
for hire or reward, carriage of goods for commercial purposes, motor racing,
speed testing, or any purpose other than that stated in the Schedule.

### 3.5 Geographical limits
Cover applies only within the geographical area stated in the Schedule.

### 3.6 Tyres and tubes
Damage to tyres and tubes is excluded unless the vehicle is damaged at the same
time, in which case liability is limited to 50 percent of replacement cost.

### 3.7 Delayed notification
The Company reserves the right to decline any claim not notified within 48
hours of the incident, save where the Insured demonstrates that notification
within that period was not reasonably possible.

## SECTION 4 - CLAIMS PROCEDURE

### 4.1 Notification
The Insured shall notify the Company immediately upon becoming aware of any
event likely to give rise to a claim.

### 4.2 Police report
For claims involving theft, malicious damage, riot, or third party injury, a
First Information Report lodged with the police is required before settlement.

### 4.3 Inspection
The Company reserves the right to inspect the damaged vehicle before repairs
commence. Repairs undertaken without prior approval may be settled at the
Company's assessment of reasonable cost rather than the invoiced amount.

## SECTION 5 - FRAUD

### 5.1 Fraudulent claims
If any claim is in any respect fraudulent, or if any fraudulent means are used
to obtain benefit under this policy, all benefit under this policy shall be
forfeited.

## SECTION 6 - DEPRECIATION SCHEDULE

### 6.1 Rubber, plastic, and nylon parts
Depreciation of 50 percent applies to all rubber, plastic, nylon, and tubeless
tyre parts, and to batteries and air bags.

### 6.2 Metal parts
Depreciation on metal parts is applied by vehicle age: nil below six months,
5 percent from six months to one year, 10 percent from one to two years,
15 percent from two to three years, 25 percent from three to four years,
35 percent from four to five years, and 50 percent thereafter.
